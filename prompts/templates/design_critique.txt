# Design Critique & Analysis

Analyze the following design: {image_or_description}

Analysis Framework:

## 1. Visual Hierarchy
- What draws the eye first? Why?
- Is the hierarchy clear and intentional?
- Rate: 1-10

## 2. Color Usage
- Color harmony and relationships
- Contrast and readability
- Emotional impact of color choices
- Rate: 1-10

## 3. Typography
- Font choices and pairing
- Readability and legibility
- Scale and rhythm
- Rate: 1-10

## 4. Layout & Composition
- Balance and alignment
- Use of white space
- Grid adherence
- Rate: 1-10

## 5. Accessibility
- WCAG contrast compliance
- Text size adequacy
- Color-blind safety
- Rate: 1-10

## 6. Overall Assessment
- Strengths (top 3)
- Weaknesses (top 3)
- Specific improvement suggestions
- Overall score: 1-10

Be specific, actionable, and constructive. Reference real design principles.
