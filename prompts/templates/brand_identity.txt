# Brand Identity Design

Design a complete brand identity for: {brand_name}

Industry: {industry}
Target Audience: {audience}
Brand Values: {values}
Mood/Tone: {mood}

Deliverables:
1. Logo concept description (3 variations)
2. Primary color palette (5 colors with hex codes)
3. Secondary color palette (3 colors with hex codes)
4. Typography pairing (heading + body font recommendations)
5. Brand voice description (3-5 adjectives with examples)
6. Visual style guide (patterns, textures, imagery style)
7. Mockup suggestions (where the brand appears)

Format as a structured brand bible document.
